== KNOWN ISSUES ==
- RESOLVED 2026-10-13 — "FFmpeg is not installed or not in your PATH" →
  transcription failed. A GUI app launched from Finder / /Applications inherits
  LaunchServices' minimal PATH (/usr/bin:/bin:/usr/sbin:/sbin), which omits
  Homebrew, so parakeet-mlx/mlx-whisper couldn't find ffmpeg. (Worked when
  launched from a terminal that had Homebrew on PATH, hence the intermittent
  "broken again".) Fix: MLXSidecar.childEnvironment() now prepends
  /opt/homebrew/bin, /usr/local/bin, and the venv's bin to the sidecar's PATH,
  so ffmpeg resolves regardless of launch context. Locked in by MLXSidecarTests.
- RESOLVED 2026-10-18 — transcription not saved to History when the paste
  failed. DictationController.process() saved to History only *after* a
  successful insertion and returned early on insertion errors, so a failed paste
  lost the words. Fix: the final text is now saved to History unconditionally,
  with a per-entry `inserted: Bool`; un-inserted entries show a "Not inserted"
  badge and can be copied / re-inserted. Backward-compatible decode; locked in by
  HistoryStoreTests. (ecf5c9b)
- Ad-hoc signing resets macOS permission grants on every rebuild (the signature
  changes, so TCC treats it as a new app). Symptom: "records but no text" or
  "constantly asks for Accessibility" even when it looks granted (stale entry
  bound to the old signature). HANDLED 2026-10-18 — `scripts/install.sh` now runs
  `tccutil reset Accessibility|Microphone com.voicedictator.app` after signing, so
  each rebuild clears the dead entry and the next launch re-prompts cleanly; just
  relaunch and allow. (A stale entry from before this change must be removed in
  System Settings with "–" and re-added.) Accepted tradeoff — signing identity
  intentionally dropped (owner reconfirmed ad-hoc 2026-10-18). (e90d3da)
